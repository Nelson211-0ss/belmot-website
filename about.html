About Belmot Microfinance

Empowering Communities Through Finance

Based in Magwi County, Eastern Equatoria, Belmot Microfinance Company is dedicated to giving low-income earners, small business owners and farmers access to fair and reliable financial services that traditional banks often overlook.

We combine affordable credit with savings products and practical financial training, so our clients can grow their incomes and build lasting financial security.

Fast loan approval with minimal paperwork

Flexible repayment plans that fit your cash flow

Free financial literacy training for every client
